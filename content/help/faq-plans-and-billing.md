# Plans, Billing & Account FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most. For anything not covered here, [contact us](/contact).

## What plans are there?

| | Free | Starter | Unlimited |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Price | ₹0 | ₹249/month | ₹449/month |
| Yearly | — | ₹2,490/year | ₹4,490/year |
| Users | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Leads | 300 | 5,000 | Unlimited |
| Lead sources | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |

| Sequences | 1 | 10 | Unlimited |
| AI credits / month | 15 | 300 | 2,000 |

Prices exclude 18% GST, and yearly plans include 2 months free. See the full comparison on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Is pricing per user?
No. You pay one flat price for your workspace. Starter covers up to 3 users; Unlimited covers as many as you like.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every new workspace gets a **14-day Starter trial** with no card required. When it ends, you move to the Free plan automatically unless you subscribe — nothing is deleted.

## What counts toward my seat limit?
Active team members and invitations that haven't been accepted yet. Deactivated and deleted team members don't count, so you never pay for past staff.

## What counts toward my lead limit?
Every lead in your workspace, whatever its status. Leads in the recycle bin don't count. At the limit nothing is deleted — you just can't add new leads until you upgrade.

## What's an AI credit?
One AI generation — a reply draft, a lead summary, an AI Assistant answer, a generated sequence or an improved message. Credits reset on the 1st of each month. If the AI fails, the credit is refunded.

## What happens if I downgrade?
Your data stays. If you have more automations or sequences than the new plan allows, the oldest ones keep running and the rest pause until you upgrade again.

## Can I change or cancel my plan?
Yes, any time from **Settings → Billing**. When you cancel, you keep your paid plan until the end of the period you paid for, then move to Free. See our [Cancellation & Refund Policy](/refund-policy).

## What payment methods do you accept?
UPI, credit and debit cards, and net banking, processed securely by Razorpay. Your card details are never stored on our servers.

## Do I get a GST invoice?
Yes. Add your GSTIN and business details in **Settings → Billing** and every payment gets a GST invoice — view, print or save it as a PDF from Settings → Billing. A copy is also emailed to you, with the invoice PDF attached.

## Do you accept coupon codes?
Yes — enter it at checkout in Settings → Billing. If you run ads for clients, [contact us](/contact) about complimentary plans for agency workspaces.

## What if a payment fails?
You get a grace period and reminder emails to update your payment method before the workspace is moved to Free. Your leads are never deleted.

## Will you remind me before my plan renews?
Yes — we email your workspace admins a few days before a renewal (or before a cancelled plan ends), and warn you as you approach your AI-credit, lead or seat limits. You can switch those reminders off from the link in the email.

## Are WhatsApp Business API charges included?
No. Personal 1-tap WhatsApp is free. If you use the official WhatsApp Business API, Meta's conversation charges are billed by your WhatsApp provider, separately from Ridhzo.

## Is my data secure?
Yes. Every workspace is kept completely separate, sensitive secrets are encrypted, and deleted leads stay in a 30-day recycle bin. Read more on our [Security page](/security).
