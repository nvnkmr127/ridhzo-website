# Plans, Billing & Account FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most about plans and billing. For anything not covered here, [contact us](/contact).

## Is there a free trial?

Yes — you can start with a free trial and explore the core workflow (lead capture, alerts, WhatsApp follow-ups, and the pipeline) before you pay.

## How does pricing work?

Ridhzo is priced per user, so it scales with your team. Agencies and larger teams have options built for multiple sub-teams. See current pricing on the [homepage](/#pricing).

## Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from **Settings → Billing** at any time. See our [Cancellation & Refund Policy](/refund-policy) for details on timing and refunds.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We support major cards and popular regional payment methods through our payment processor. Your billing details are handled securely and never stored on our servers.

## Is my data secure?

Yes. Ridhzo uses AES-256 encryption and strict tenant isolation, so your data is never exposed to other workspaces. Read more on our [Security page](/security).

## What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data remains available during your billing period. You can export your leads before canceling. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for how we handle data retention and deletion.

## How do I get support?

- Check the [Help Center](/help) and [How-To Guides](/how-to).
- [Contact us](/contact) — we respond within 24 hours.
