# How to Respond to Inbound Leads in Under 5 Minutes

Fast response is the single highest-leverage change most sales teams can make. This guide walks through the setup that makes a sub-5-minute first response the default, not the exception.

## Step 1 — Get every lead into one place

Connect all your sources so nothing slips through:

- Meta Lead Ads ([setup guide](/how-to/connect-meta-lead-ads))
- Website and landing-page forms
- Webhooks from other tools
- CSV import for existing lists

## Step 2 — Turn on instant alerts

Enable **mobile push notifications** so the assigned rep feels a vibrating alert the moment a lead arrives. Email alone is too slow — it gets checked in batches.

## Step 3 — Automate assignment

Enable **round-robin routing** so each new lead is instantly assigned to an available rep. No lead should wait for someone to manually claim it.

## Step 4 — Make first contact one tap

Set up **WhatsApp templates** in Settings → Templates. These fill in automatically: `{{first_name}}`, `{{name}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{phone}}`, `{{company}}`.

> Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for your enquiry! Is now a good time for a quick chat?

With the WhatsApp Business API, an automation can even send this the moment the lead arrives.

Now first contact is a single tap — the chat opens pre-filled with the prospect's number and your greeting.

## Step 5 — Watch your response time

The **Executive Dashboard** tracks it for you:

- Average and median time to first response.
- The % of leads contacted within 5 minutes.
- Leads that missed the 15-minute response target.
- The **Going Cold** list flags leads nobody has contacted for 14 days.

## Step 6 — Review weekly

Check first-response times per rep each week. Celebrate the fast ones, coach the slow ones. Speed-to-lead is a habit, and the scoreboard is what keeps it sharp.

With this in place, responding in minutes stops being heroic effort and becomes how your team simply operates.
