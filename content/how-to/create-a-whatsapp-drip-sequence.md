# How to Create a WhatsApp & Email Drip Sequence

Most leads aren't ready to buy on day one. A sequence keeps in touch for you over days or weeks until they are, and it stops as soon as they reply.

## Step 1: Describe your goal and let AI draft it
Open **Sequences**. In the builder, type a goal in plain English, for example:

> *Nurture a new real estate lead over two weeks toward booking an on-site property tour.*

Click **Generate**. Ridhzo drafts the steps, day offsets, channels and message copy.

## Step 2: Review every step
For each step, check:
- **Day offset**: e.g. Day 0, Day 2, Day 5, Day 9, counted from enrollment
- **Channel**: WhatsApp or Email
- **Message**: edit the copy and keep tokens like `{{first_name}}`
- **Attachment** *(optional)*: add a brochure, floor plan or price sheet link

Tip: open with value (brochure, video, pricing) rather than "just checking in".

## Step 3: Check quiet hours
In **Settings**, confirm your **timezone** and **quiet hours** (e.g. 8 PM–9 AM). Steps due inside that window are held until the morning.

## Step 4: Save
Save the sequence. It now appears in your list with its step count and number of active enrollments.

## Step 5: Enroll leads
Pick one or both:
- **Manually**: on a lead profile, tap **+ Add to Sequence** and choose the sequence.
- **Automatically**: add an **Enroll in sequence** action to an automation ([guide](/how-to/build-your-first-automation)) so every new ad lead starts the cadence.

## Step 6: Watch it run
Open the sequence to see its flow: how many leads are waiting on each step, what share of the total that is, and how many finished or were removed early.

## What stops a sequence automatically
- The lead **replies on WhatsApp**
- The lead **replies by email** (when inbound email is connected)
- A rep marks the deal **Won, Lost or Unqualified**

A rep can also tap **Stop** on the lead profile. Every stop is logged on the timeline.

## Edit live sequences safely
Improve the copy at any time. Leads already enrolled keep their place and get your updated steps from then on.
