# Android Call Sync & Caller ID

## Stop typing call reports at 7 PM
Reps make dozens of calls a day. In most CRMs they have to remember to log each one — so most never get logged, and managers fly blind. With the Ridhzo Android app, calls made from the rep's own phone are logged **automatically**.

## What gets logged
- **Direction** — outgoing, incoming or missed
- **Talk time** — exact duration in seconds
- **When** — start and end time
- **Which lead** — the number is matched to the lead automatically
- **Outcome** — the rep can add a note and how the call went, saved on the lead's timeline

All of it appears on the lead's timeline and feeds your dashboards, so **call volume, talk time and answer rate** are measured without anyone filling in a sheet.

## Follow-ups that close themselves
- **Answered call** — when a call to or from a lead connects, matching pending follow-ups are marked **Completed**.
- **Unanswered call** — a missed or unanswered attempt is logged, but the follow-up stays **open**, so the rep remembers to try again.

## Smart missed-call handling
When a lead rings a rep and the call is missed, the phone already showed its own missed-call alert. Ridhzo avoids sending a duplicate alert to that phone and still logs the missed call on the lead's timeline, so nothing is lost.

## Caller ID for your leads (opt-in)
Leads often call from numbers that aren't saved in the rep's contacts. Turn on Caller ID in the app and, when one of your leads rings, the incoming-call alert shows **their name and where the deal stands** — for example "Interested · ₹1.5L" — before the rep answers. Reps only see leads they're allowed to open.

## Works with automations
A **call logged** trigger lets you build rules — two ready-made templates come built in:
- *Three unanswered calls in a row → add a note and schedule a follow-up at a different time of day*
- *A call longer than two minutes → mark the lead as in progress*

## You stay in control
- The app asks for **call-log and phone permission** on the rep's Android phone — nothing is read until it's granted.
- **Personal calls stay on the phone.** The app matches its call log against your leads' numbers and sends only those calls — the rest never leave the device.
- A rep only syncs calls for leads they can open (admins: all of the workspace's leads), and read-only roles can't sync.
- It is available on **every plan, including Free**.
- Call logging is built for the Android app. iPhone users can install the web app, but automatic call logging needs Android.

## Real example
A real-estate rep calls a lead from her phone at the time a follow-up was due. The call runs four minutes; Ridhzo logs it, marks the follow-up done and updates "last contacted". A second lead doesn't pick up: the attempt is logged, the follow-up stays open on her list. The owner's dashboard shows both without anyone typing a note.
