# Follow-ups, Reminders & Calendar

## Deals are won in the follow-up
Most sales need five or more touchpoints, yet many reps give up after one. When follow-ups live on sticky notes and in memory, deals slip away quietly. Ridhzo ties every follow-up to the lead record.

---

## Your daily follow-up list
The **Follow-ups** page sorts every commitment into a clear queue:
- **Overdue**: promises that have slipped
- **Due today**: what you need to do before end of day
- **Upcoming**: what's coming next

Mark a task done in **one click**, or snooze it to 9 AM tomorrow (**+1d**) or later in the week (**+3d**) with one tap.

## Month calendar view
Switch to the calendar to see how many follow-ups fall on each day, spot overdue items and jump straight to the lead from any entry.

## Four reminder types
Schedule each touchpoint as a **Call**, **WhatsApp**, **Email** or **Meeting**, each with its own icon so your day is easy to scan.

---

## Reminders that find you
Ridhzo checks your schedule in the background and alerts you **15 minutes before** each follow-up, so you're never caught unprepared.

## Automatic escalation
Overdue follow-ups are ranked by how late they are:

| Severity | Overdue by |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Medium** | Under 24 hours |
| **High** | 24–48 hours |
| **Critical** | 48 hours or more |

Escalations appear on the lead's timeline and on the manager's dashboard. You can also trigger [automations](/features/automations) on *Follow-up overdue*.

## Pipeline stays in sync
Completing a follow-up records a customer contact automatically, which resets the lead's *Going Cold* timer. The lead profile, pipeline card and follow-up list always point to the same next task, so you never see stale overdue alerts.

## Google Calendar sync
Booking requests create a follow-up task automatically and add a 30-minute slot to the rep's Google Calendar.

---
