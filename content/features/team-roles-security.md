# Team, Roles & Security

## Bring your team in
- **Invite by email** — teammates set a password or sign in with Google.
- **Sign-in options:** email & password, Google, or phone number with OTP.
- **Teams** — group people by city, product or language for lead rotation and reports.
- **Deactivate** anyone in one click — they stop receiving leads and their history stays.

## Roles & permissions
Use the built-in **Admin** and **Member** roles, or create your own — for example a read-only **Viewer** for a partner or accountant. Choose from 14 permissions:

- Manage users & teams · Manage roles
- Edit organisation settings · Manage lead sources & assignment
- Manage templates · Manage automations · Manage sequences
- Edit leads · Delete leads · Permanently delete leads · Merge duplicates
- View audit log · Manage API keys, webhooks & alerts · Manage billing

**Reps see only their own leads.** A sales rep sees and works only the leads assigned to them (and leads they have a meeting with). Admins see everything. No more leads leaking out of a shared spreadsheet.

Permissions are checked on our servers, not just hidden in the screen.

## Audit log
See who changed settings, roles and users, who deleted or merged leads, who created API keys — with the time and the person.

## How we protect your data
- Every workspace is **completely separate** — no other business can see your leads.
- Sensitive secrets (email passwords, API keys, connection tokens) are **encrypted**.
- API keys are stored securely and can be read-only.
- Webhooks are **signed**, and public forms are protected from spam.
- Deleted leads stay in a **30-day recycle bin**.
- A lead's data can be **exported or erased** on request.
