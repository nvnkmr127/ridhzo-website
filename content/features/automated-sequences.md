# No-Code Automations: When → If → Then

## Why automations matter
Replying to an inbound lead within 5 minutes makes you far more likely to qualify it. Most teams still lose hours copying lead details, pinging reps and typing the same welcome message. Ridhzo Automations handle that busywork as soon as the lead arrives, day or night.

---

## The visual rule builder
Every automation reads like a sentence. You don't need to write code or learn a flowchart tool.

### WHEN: the trigger
Choose the event that starts the workflow:

| Trigger | Fires when… |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lead created** | A lead arrives from Meta Lead Ads, Google Lead Forms, a web form, the API, a CSV import or manual entry |
| **Lead assigned** | A lead gets a new owner |
| **Lead status changed** | A lead moves between lifecycle states (e.g. *New → Won*) |
| **Stage changed** | A card is moved on the pipeline board |
| **Tag added** | A rep or an import attaches a tag |
| **Follow-up scheduled / completed / overdue** | A follow-up task is booked, done or past its deadline |
| **Task completed** | A general task is finished |

### IF: the conditions (optional)
Narrow the rule to the leads that matter:
- **Lead source** such as *Facebook Ads only* or *Website Consultation Form*
- **Any lead field**: status, company, email, phone, expected value
- **Tags and custom fields** such as `budget greater than 50,000` or `industry equals Healthcare`
- **Operators**: equals, not equals, contains, does not contain, greater than, less than
- **AND / OR groups** for complex logic

### THEN: the actions, run in order
- **Assign lead** to a specific rep
- **Round-robin balance** to the rep with the most free capacity
- **Change status** automatically
- **Schedule follow-up** with relative timing (in *X* days, hours or minutes)
- **Create task** as an internal to-do
- **Add note** to the lead's timeline
- **Send WhatsApp** as an approved template with `{{name}}` personalisation
- **Enroll in sequence** to start a multi-day WhatsApp and email drip

## 1-click templates
Start with one of four ready-made workflows:
1. **Welcome WhatsApp on new lead**: an instant, personalised greeting sent within seconds.
2. **Schedule a first follow-up**: every new lead gets a follow-up task 24 hours out.
3. **Nudge on overdue follow-ups**: an alert note appears when a promise is missed.
4. **Ask for a referral on won deals**: a reminder to thank the buyer and ask for introductions.

---

## Example: zero-touch Meta Ads intake
1. A prospect submits your Instagram lead form.
2. **IF** the source is *Meta Ads*:
   - Round-robin assigns the lead to the rep with the most free capacity.
   - A WhatsApp welcome goes out in under 30 seconds.
   - A follow-up call is booked for 9 AM tomorrow.
   - The lead is enrolled in your *7-Day Product Nurture* sequence.

## Example: high-value escalation
**WHEN** a lead is created, **IF** `budget greater than 50,000`, **THEN** assign it to your senior closer, add the note *"High-budget inbound. Priority response required"* and book a follow-up within the hour.

## Built to be safe at scale
- **Loop protection**: automations can't trigger each other in endless cycles.
- **De-duplication**: the same event never fires the same action twice.
- **Partial-failure tolerance**: one failed step doesn't silently cancel the rest.
- **Tenant isolation**: rules only ever touch your organisation's leads.
- **1-tap on/off**: pause any workflow instantly without deleting it.
