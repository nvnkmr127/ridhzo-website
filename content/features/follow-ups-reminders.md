# Follow-ups & Reminders

## Promised to call back Saturday at 11? Ridhzo remembers.
Forgotten callbacks are the most common reason leads are lost. Ridhzo makes sure every lead has a next step and every next step happens.

## What's included
- **Set a follow-up** on any lead in two taps — title, date and time, notes.
- **On-time reminders** by push notification and in-app alert (email too, if you want it).
- **Follow-ups list** — Today, Upcoming, Overdue and Done.
- **Calendar view** — see the week or month at a glance.
- **One tap to complete** — the lead's "last contacted" and "next follow-up" update automatically.
- **Overdue escalation** — overdue follow-ups are marked High (24h+) or Critical (48h+) so managers can step in.
- **Automatic follow-ups** — automations can schedule them for you ("new lead → follow up tomorrow at 10 AM").
- **After a meeting** — record the outcome and set the next follow-up in the same step.
- **Your timezone** — reminders follow your workspace timezone and working hours.
- **Morning summary** — a daily team summary so everyone starts the day knowing what's due.

## Real examples
- A lead says "call me Saturday at 11". The rep sets a follow-up; on Saturday at 11 their phone buzzes with the lead's name and a one-tap call button.
- Every site visit automatically gets a follow-up two days later.
- On Monday, a manager sees 12 critical overdue follow-ups for one rep and reassigns them in bulk.
