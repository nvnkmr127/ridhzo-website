# AI Sales Copilot

## A sales assistant on every screen
Tap the sparkle button in the bottom-right corner of any Ridhzo screen to open the copilot. It knows which page you're on. On a lead's profile it already has that lead's details, score and history loaded.

---

## Ask it anything about your pipeline
Starter prompts on the dashboard:
- *"What should I focus on today?"*
- *"Show hot leads that have gone cold"*
- *"Which leads haven't been contacted in 7 days?"*
- *"Draft a follow-up for my newest lead"*

Prompts on a lead profile:
- *"Draft a follow-up message for this lead"*
- *"What's the next best action for this lead?"*
- *"Summarize this lead's activity"*
- *"Set a reminder to follow up in 3 days"*

---

## It does the admin for you
The copilot can take real, reversible actions in your CRM:

| Action | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Find leads** | Search by name, phone, email, company or status |
| **Read a lead** | Pull the full profile and the 15 latest timeline events |
| **Change status** | Move a lead and log the reason |
| **Add a tag** | Segment leads for filtering |
| **Set a reminder** | Schedule a follow-up at a specific time |
| **Assign a lead** | Hand it to a teammate |

## You stay in control of outreach
The copilot **never auto-sends** WhatsApp messages or emails. When it drafts a message, it shows a card with the channel, recipient and text, plus **Send** and **Dismiss** buttons. Nothing reaches a prospect until you tap Send.

---

## AI across the rest of Ridhzo
- **AI lead recap**: a summary of the full lead history before you pick up the phone.
- **AI reply drafting**: suggested WhatsApp replies inside the conversation thread.
- **AI sequence drafting**: a one-line goal becomes a complete [drip sequence](/features/sequences).
- **Business context**: add your offerings, target customers and tone in Settings so every draft sounds like your team.

## Private by design
- Conversations are stored per user, so reps never see each other's chats.
- Each action is checked against your organisation on the server. The AI can't reach another company's data.
- Each request is limited to a small number of steps, so the AI can't run up costs or take unexpected actions.
- If advanced AI features are unavailable, the copilot still answers in plain conversation instead of failing.
