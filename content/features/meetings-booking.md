# Meetings, Site Visits & Booking Page

## The meeting is where the deal is won
Ridhzo handles everything around it — booking, confirming, reminding, showing up and recording what happened.

## Meeting types
- **Online meeting** — Google Meet or Zoom; the join link is sent to the lead.
- **Site visit** — property viewing, rooftop survey, interior measurement.
- **Store / office visit** — showroom, clinic, campus, office.
- **In person** — at the customer's place.

Durations from 15 minutes to 2 hours.

## What's included
- **Schedule from the lead page** — type, time, duration, who's going, place or link.
- **Saved locations** — your offices, stores and project sites with address and Google Maps pin.
- **Confirmation to the lead** on WhatsApp and/or email — time in their local timezone, address, map link and your rep's name.
- **Automatic reminders** — the lead gets reminders **24 hours** and **1 hour** before; the rep gets a heads-up before the meeting and a prompt afterwards to record the outcome.
- **Google Calendar** — meetings sync to your calendar when connected, or add them with one tap.
- **GPS check-in** — field reps check in on arrival; time and location are saved.
- **Outcome in one step** — Completed, No-show or Cancelled, a note, and the next follow-up.
- **Custom message templates** for confirmations and reminders.

## Public booking page
Share a link like **app.ridhzo.com/book/your-business** in your Instagram bio, Google Business profile or WhatsApp. Leads pick a **30-minute slot** within your working days and hours, up to **14 days ahead**. The booking creates the lead and the meeting automatically.

## Real examples
- **Real estate:** Site visit booked → WhatsApp confirmation with Google Maps pin → reminders the day before and an hour before → rep checks in at the site → outcome "Interested in Tower B, 3BHK" → follow-up in 2 days.
- **Clinic:** Patients book consultations from Instagram at night; the front desk sees them in the morning.
- **No-show:** Mark it, and a follow-up to reschedule is set in the same step.
