# AI Assistant, Reply Drafts & Lead Summaries

## AI where it actually saves time
Reading a long history, writing the next message, updating the CRM — that's where reps lose hours. Ridhzo's AI does those jobs. **It never messages a customer on its own; a person always approves.**

## AI Assistant
Open it from any screen and ask in plain language:
- "Show me today's new leads from Facebook."
- "What's happening with Ramesh Kumar?"
- "Move Priya to Site Visit Booked and remind me to call her Friday at 5 PM."
- "Assign all of Anil's untouched leads to Sneha."
- "Book a site visit with Asha tomorrow at 11 at Green Acres."
- "Draft a WhatsApp to Rahul about the new prices."

It can search leads (by name, phone, email or company), read their history, change status, add tags, set reminders, assign leads, book meetings and draft messages for your approval. Conversations are saved so you can pick up later.

## Lead summary with one-tap suggestions
One click gives you who the lead is, what they want, where things stand and the single best next step — perfect before a call or when taking over a colleague's lead.

Along with the summary, Ridhzo proposes changes you can accept or dismiss:
- **Fill in details** it found in the conversation — budget, preferred location, timeline and other fields
- **Move to the next status** when the conversation has reached it
- **Next step** — for example "site visit on Saturday"

Each is applied with a single tap, and a dismissed suggestion doesn't come back. The summary is saved on the lead, so reopening it costs nothing until something on the lead changes. The mobile app shows the saved summary without spending a credit.

## Pre-call brief — free
Before you dial, open the **pre-call brief** on the lead: your last contact and when it happened, the latest note, the details still missing that you should ask about, the next step and any upcoming meeting. It's built instantly from the lead's own history — no AI credit used.

## Guidance that follows *your* process
- **Status playbooks** — an admin writes a few lines for each status ("Site visit booked: confirm the day before, send the location pin…"). Ridhzo's AI then follows the playbook of the lead's current status, so its advice matches how your team sells.
- **AI business profile** — describe your business, products, audience and tone once, so drafts and suggestions sound like you and stick to your offer.

## Draft with AI
On any lead, get the next WhatsApp or email written for you:
- Based on the lead's form answers and your conversation so far.
- **Tone:** friendly, professional or short — or let Ridhzo choose one that fits the conversation.
- **Language:** matches the lead automatically, or choose Hindi, Hinglish, Telugu, Tamil and more.
- Never invents prices, offers or dates.
