# AI Assistant, Reply Drafts & Lead Summaries

## AI where it actually saves time
Reading a long history, writing the next message, updating the CRM — that's where reps lose hours. Ridhzo's AI does those jobs. **It never messages a customer on its own; a person always approves.**

## AI Assistant
Open it from any screen and ask in plain language:
- "Show me today's new leads from Facebook."
- "What's happening with Ramesh Kumar?"
- "Move Priya to Site Visit Booked and remind me to call her Friday at 5 PM."
- "Assign all of Anil's untouched leads to Sneha."
- "Book a site visit with Asha tomorrow at 11 at Green Acres."
- "Draft a WhatsApp to Rahul about the new prices."

It can search leads, read their history, change status, add tags, set reminders, assign leads, book meetings and draft messages for your approval. Conversations are saved so you can pick up later.

## Draft with AI
On any lead, get the next WhatsApp or email written for you:
- Based on the lead's form answers and your conversation so far.
- **Tone:** friendly, professional or short.
- **Language:** matches the lead automatically, or choose Hindi, Hinglish, Telugu, Tamil and more.
- Never invents prices, offers or dates.

## Lead summary
One click gives you who the lead is, what they want, where things stand and what to do next — perfect before a call or when taking over a colleague's lead.

## Sequences from one sentence
Describe your goal and AI writes a complete follow-up sequence with timing and messages.

## Auto-tagging replies (Starter & Unlimited)
Incoming WhatsApp replies are tagged by intent — interested, price question, not interested — so your team knows who to call first.

- **Next best action** on every lead
- **Best time to contact**
- **Going Cold** detection with a win-back plan
