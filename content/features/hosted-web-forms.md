# Hosted Web Forms & Website Embeds

## Replace your form builder and your middleware
Third-party form tools cost money and add delay: form → Zapier → spreadsheet → CRM. Ridhzo forms are built into the CRM, so a submission becomes a lead the moment it's sent.

---

## Build it visually
- **Add, rename, reorder and delete** fields in the inline editor.
- **Field types**: text, email, phone, number and long text.
- **Required toggles** checked in the browser and on the server.
- Anything beyond name, email, phone and company is saved as **custom data** on the lead automatically.

## Multi-step forms that convert
Long single-page forms get abandoned. Split yours into **up to 10 steps**, e.g. *Step 1: Contact info → Step 2: Requirements*:
- Progress indicator and **Next / Back** navigation
- Answers are kept between steps
- Each step must be valid before the lead can move on

## Two ways to publish
### Hosted link
A fast, mobile-friendly page at `/f/<form-id>`. Use it in:
- Instagram and LinkedIn bios
- WhatsApp messages and email signatures
- QR codes on flyers, site boards and event stands

### Embed on your website
Copy one `<iframe>` snippet and paste it into **WordPress, Elementor, Webflow, Framer, Squarespace, Shopify** or plain HTML. The form runs in a sandbox, so it won't clash with your site's styles.

---

## What happens on submit
1. Ridhzo checks there's at least an email or phone number.
2. Core fields map to the lead, and every other answer is saved as custom data.
3. Duplicates are caught by phone and email.
4. Assignment rules, [automations](/features/automations) and [sequences](/features/sequences) fire, and the owner's phone gets an alert.
