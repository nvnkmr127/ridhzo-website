# Custom Fields, Stages & Message Templates

Every business qualifies leads differently. Real estate tracks *property type* and *budget*, a driving school tracks *licence category*, and B2B SaaS tracks *tech stack* and *contract value*. Ridhzo adapts to your process.

---

## Custom fields
### 10 validated data types
| Type | Good for |
| :--- | :--- |
| Text / Long text | Notes, addresses, requirements |
| Number / Currency | Budget, team size, deal value (₹ $ € £ symbols handled) |
| Date / Date & time | Move-in date, policy renewal, test-drive slot |
| Select / Multi-select | Property type, product interest, locations |
| Checkbox | Loan pre-approved? Site visited? |
| URL | Listing link, LinkedIn profile |

Values are checked and cleaned automatically, so your reports add up.

### Tidy, tabbed profiles
Group fields into **tabs and sub-tabs** (e.g. *Requirements → Budget & Timeline*) so profiles don't turn into one long scroll.

### Show on the leads table
Mark any field **Show on table** and it becomes a column in the [Leads Hub](/features/leads-hub).

### Admin-only fields
Hide sensitive data such as *commission split*, *credit score* or *internal margin* from reps. It is removed on the server, not just hidden on screen.

### Required fields
Make a field required so reps can't skip important details.

## Your own pipeline stages
- Rename stages and choose their **colors and categories** (open, won or lost)
- Ridhzo blocks changes that would leave leads without a stage or break automations
- **Time-in-stage stats** (average and median hours) show where deals slow down

## Message templates
Save your best messages and send them in one tap on **WhatsApp**, **Email** and **SMS**.
- Personalisation tokens: `{{first_name}}`, `{{name}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{phone}}`, `{{company}}`
- Used everywhere: lead profiles, quick replies, [sequences](/features/sequences) and [automations](/features/automations)
- Only admins can edit templates, so every rep sends the same approved message

## Workspace settings
- **Timezone and currency** applied to schedules, reports and deal values
- **Quiet hours** so automated messages never go out at night
- **AI business context**: describe your offerings, customers and tone so AI drafts sound like you
- **Send email from your own domain** via SMTP. Passwords are encrypted, and you can send a test email first.
- **Audit trail**: every settings change records who changed what, from what to what
