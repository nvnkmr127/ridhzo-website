# Leads Hub: Smart Segments, Bulk Actions & Import

## Built for high-volume inbound
Leads lose value fast. When they sit in spreadsheets or slow CRMs, duplicates pile up and reps waste hours updating records one by one. The Leads Hub lets your team find, sort and act on hundreds of leads in seconds.

---

## Always up to date
The list **refreshes itself every 20 seconds** while you're looking at it and updates as soon as you return to the tab. New webhook leads appear without a page reload.

## Smart segments with one tap
Live-count chips filter the list instantly:
- 🔥 **Hot leads**: high-intent leads with recent engagement
- ⚠️ **High value at risk**: big deals ageing without contact
- 👤 **Unassigned new**: fresh leads with no owner
- ❄️ **Stale high priority**: important deals that have [gone cold](/features/pipeline-kanban)

## Find anything
- **Instant search** across name, email, company and phone. Phone search ignores spaces, dashes and country codes.
- **Filter builder** with AND / OR rules on status, owner, source, tag, priority, score, expected value, dates and more
- **Meta ad filters**: campaign, ad set, ad and form
- **Saved views** such as *My Active Deals* or *Q3 High Value*
- **Sort** by created date, updated date, next follow-up, priority, score and more

## A flexible table
- Short lead IDs like **#1042** that are easy to say on the phone
- Status badges that match your [custom stages](/features/custom-fields-templates)
- Any custom field can be shown as a column. Admin-only fields stay hidden from reps.
- A **next best action** badge on every row

---

## Bulk actions
Select leads and then:
- **Assign** them to any teammate
- **Change status** in one go
- **Tag** the whole selection
- **Broadcast on WhatsApp** with `{{first_name}}` personalisation
- **Export to CSV**
- **Delete** them to the recycle bin (restorable for 30 days)

## Import from a spreadsheet
The 4-step wizard:
1. **Upload** a CSV (a sample template is provided)
2. **Map** columns to standard or custom fields
3. **Dry run**: preview new records, errors and duplicates before anything is saved
4. **Commit**, with a lead source and default owner applied

## Quick add, even offline
Add a walk-in or phone lead in under 5 seconds from anywhere in the app. Your custom fields are included, and if the signal drops, the lead is [saved offline](/features/offline-mode) and synced when you reconnect.

## Global search (⌘K)
Press **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** anywhere to search leads and teammates and jump to them from the keyboard.
