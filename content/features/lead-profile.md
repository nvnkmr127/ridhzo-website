# 360° Lead Profile & Buying Signals

## Stop calling blind
Reps often forget what a prospect said three days ago, or whether they've opened the proposal. The Ridhzo lead profile puts the whole relationship on one screen, from the first ad click to the latest WhatsApp reply.

---

## What's on the profile
- **Quick-action bar**: Call, WhatsApp, Email, Follow-up, Edit
- **Next best action**: a recommendation based on lead score, buying signals and your SLA window
- **AI recap**: a 3-second summary of the full history ([AI Copilot](/features/ai-assistant))
- **Qualification details** and your [custom fields](/features/custom-fields-templates), grouped into tabs
- **Follow-up widget** with one-click scheduling presets
- **Sequences card**: enroll in or stop a [drip sequence](/features/sequences)
- **Tabs** for Activity, WhatsApp, Notes, Follow-ups and Attachments

## Know the moment they open your proposal
Share proposals, brochures and quotes as **trackable links**:
- Create a link for any document or page in seconds.
- Copy it or share it straight to WhatsApp.
- See **view counts and read receipts** in real time.
- When a lead opens your content, a **buying signal banner** appears: *"Opened 'Pricing Deck' 3 times recently. Reach out now while you're top of mind."*

## WhatsApp, your way
- **Personal mode**: one-tap `wa.me` routing from your own WhatsApp, with no Meta approval needed.
- **Business mode**: WhatsApp Cloud API with the full two-way thread saved on the lead.
- **AI reply drafting** and one-tap message templates in both modes.

## See exactly where the lead came from
For Meta leads, see the **campaign, ad set, ad and form name**. Full **UTM parameters** are captured for every source, so you know which ads bring in revenue.

---

## Helpful warnings
- **Duplicate warning** when another lead has the same phone or email
- **Lost reason banner** on closed-lost leads
- **30-day recycle bin**: deleted leads can be restored for 30 days

## A typical flow
1. A new Meta lead arrives. The rep opens the profile and reads the AI recap.
2. They tap WhatsApp, send a template, and move the status to *Active*.
3. They share a trackable brochure link and schedule a follow-up for Thursday at 10 AM.
4. On Wednesday a buying-signal banner appears because the lead opened the brochure twice. The rep calls while the lead is still interested.
