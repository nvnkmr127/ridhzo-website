# Lead Management: Search, Filters & Lead Profile

## Find any lead instantly
- **Search** by name, phone, email or company. Phone numbers work in any format; email works by full address, part of it, or domain (`@company.com`).
- **Global search** from anywhere with **Ctrl/⌘ + K**.
- **Filters** on status, owner, team, source, tags, dates, score and any custom field — combine with AND / OR.
- **Sort** by newest, last updated, name, status, owner, next follow-up or score.
- **Saved views** — save filters like "My untouched Facebook leads" and open them in one tap.
- **Smart segments** — hot leads, high-value deals at risk, unassigned new leads.

## Act on hundreds of leads at once
Select leads and **assign**, **change status**, **add tags**, **send a WhatsApp campaign** (up to 500), **export to CSV** or **delete**.

## Capture what matters to your business
**Custom fields** in 10 types — text, long text, number, currency (₹, $, €, £), date, date & time, dropdown, multi-select, checkbox and link. Group them into tabs, make them required, and use them in forms, filters, imports and automations. **Tags** add quick labels like "2BHK" or "Budget 50L+".

## The lead profile — everything in one place
- Contact details, status, owner, priority, score and source
- One-tap call, WhatsApp, SMS and email
- **What they told you in the form**
- Full **timeline**: notes, calls, messages, status changes, assignments, meetings
- WhatsApp conversation (Business API)
- Follow-ups, meetings and sequences
- **Attachments** — quotes, documents, audio files
- **Shared links** and whether they were opened
- AI summary, AI reply draft, next best action and best time to contact

## Keep your data clean
- **Duplicates:** find leads with the same phone or email and merge them — notes, messages, follow-ups and tags are combined. Turn on auto-merge for new duplicates.
- **Recycle bin:** deleted leads can be restored for **30 days**.
- **Import & export:** CSV in, CSV out.

## Real example
A manager opens the saved view "Unassigned — today", selects 25 leads and assigns them across three reps in ten seconds. A rep taking over a colleague's lead reads the AI summary and timeline, and continues the conversation without asking the customer to repeat anything.
