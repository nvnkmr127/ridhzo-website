# Shared Pipeline Visibility Without Losing Role-Based Control

Collaboration breaks down at both extremes: locking reps inside isolated silos or giving every user unrestricted export access to your entire lead database.

## Structuring access as your team expands

- **Organize reps into teams.** Group specialists by region, branch or product line so routing rules and leaderboards stay focused.
- **Use custom role permissions.** Control who can view unassigned leads, edit pipeline stages, export CSVs or adjust workspace automations.
- **Keep a full audit trail.** Every status change, reassignment and note logs who made the update and when.

## Better handoffs between callers and closers

1. **Reference numbers on every lead.** Share an exact lead ID in internal discussions instead of pasting phone numbers across chat apps.
2. **Unified lead timeline.** Field closers see every call, WhatsApp message, meeting and note before walking into a site visit.
3. **Clear manager alerts.** Team leads get notified when high-priority follow-ups turn overdue so deals never slip during handoffs.

## Transparency without friction

Give reps the context they need to close faster while keeping workspace data structured and protected.
