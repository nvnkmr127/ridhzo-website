# Measuring First-Response SLA & Source ROI With Executive Dashboards

You cannot improve response speed or marketing efficiency without seeing where time and leads leak across your funnel. Clear daily metrics turn guesswork into repeatable coaching.

## Four numbers every sales leader should track

- **First-response SLA:** How many minutes pass between lead arrival and the first call or WhatsApp touchpoint per rep.
- **Source ROI:** Which ad campaigns, web forms or referral channels generate Won deals rather than low-intent form fills.
- **Call & follow-up completion:** Daily call counts, talk time and overdue follow-up backlogs across the team.
- **Win/loss & pipeline forecast:** Expected revenue by stage alongside clear reasons deals are closing or dropping off.

## Built for morning reviews and rep focus

1. **Executive Today panel & morning email.** Start every day knowing what is due, who is leading and which pipeline risks need attention.
2. **Personal rep dashboards.** Every rep gets a clear view of their own queue, overdue tasks and weekly momentum.

## Let data guide your next campaign

When marketing and sales share the same source-to-revenue dashboard, budget flows toward campaigns that actually close.
