# Capturing Leads Offline at Expos, Basements & Remote Site Visits

Field sales teams lose dozens of warm enquiries at trade shows and project sites simply because a CRM web form fails to load on weak 4G signal.

## Why paper notes and phone memos fail after events

- **Handwriting gets misread.** Misspelled emails and wrong digits turn expensive event booths into dead leads.
- **Post-event entry takes days.** By Monday afternoon when a spreadsheet is finally uploaded, prospects barely remember visiting your stall.
- **Lost context.** Quick conversations about budget, site preferences or timeline never make it onto the lead card.

## How offline outbox capture keeps field teams moving

1. **Save leads with zero signal.** Log contact details, source tags and notes straight inside the Ridhzo mobile experience even in airplane mode or basements.
2. **Automatic background sync.** As soon as your phone reconnects, queued leads sync into your workspace pipeline and trigger assigned follow-ups.
3. **GPS check-ins for scheduled visits.** Confirm site visits and store meeting notes directly on the lead record while on location.

## Never leave a booth or site empty-handed

Turn every walk-in and event conversation into a tracked pipeline opportunity from the moment you meet the buyer.
