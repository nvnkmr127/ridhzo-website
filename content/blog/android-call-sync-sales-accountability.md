# Automatic Call Logging on Android: End Manual CRM Updates

If your team sells by phone, asking reps to type every call summary into a spreadsheet guarantees incomplete records. Activity vanishes whenever the afternoon gets busy.

## Why manual call logging fails in the field

- **Reps are on the move.** Between site visits and client calls, opening a laptop form is the last thing anyone wants to do.
- **Missed callbacks go unnoticed.** Without caller context on the incoming ring, returning prospects sound like unknown numbers.
- **Managers lack true call metrics.** Counting talk time and connected calls becomes guesswork.

## How Android Call Sync keeps timelines complete

1. **Caller ID before pickup.** See the lead's name and context right when they call back so every greeting feels personal.
2. **Automatic direction and duration sync.** Outbound, inbound and missed calls attach cleanly to the matching lead timeline.
3. **One-tap follow-up completion.** When a scheduled call connects, Ridhzo marks the follow-up complete and prompts only for the next step.

## Spend hours selling, not logging

When call history stays current by default, reps save time every day and team leaderboards reflect real customer conversations.
