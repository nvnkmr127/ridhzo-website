# Connecting Google Lead Form Ads & Webhooks in Under 5 Minutes

Paid search and social ads capture buyers at peak intent, yet manual CSV exports introduce hours of lag between form submission and first contact.

## Bring every capture channel into one feed

- **Meta & Google Lead Ads.** Pull Facebook, Instagram and Google Lead Form submissions straight into Ridhzo with source and campaign tags intact.
- **Web forms & signed webhooks.** Forward enquiries from landing pages, Elementor, WordPress or custom forms via REST API and signed webhooks.
- **Missed calls & CSV imports.** Capture inbound phone enquiries and bulk lists cleanly with automatic duplicate detection and merging.

## Send conversion signals back to Meta

1. **Map pipeline stages to outcomes.** Track which ad sets produce booked site visits and won deals, not just cheap clicks.
2. **Use Meta Conversions API integration.** Feed real CRM milestone events back to your ad account to optimize campaign delivery.

## Eliminate manual spreadsheet exports

Once your sources flow automatically, every marketing rupee works harder because sales responds while the prospect is still looking at their phone.
