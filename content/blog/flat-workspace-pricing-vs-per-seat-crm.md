# Flat Workspace Pricing vs Per-Seat CRM Billing as Teams Grow

Growing sales teams often delay adding coordinators, telecallers or branch managers to their CRM because every extra login inflates the monthly bill. That workaround creates shared passwords and fractured lead tracking.

## The hidden cost of per-seat CRM plans

- **Shared logins destroy audit trails.** When three reps share one seat to save money, you lose visibility into who updated a deal or called a lead.
- **Seasonal hiring gets penalized.** Adding temporary staff during peak campaigns triggers immediate subscription upgrades.
- **Managers sit outside the workflow.** Operations and leadership rely on exported CSVs instead of checking live pipeline dashboards.

## What changes with flat per-workspace pricing

1. **Free, Starter and Unlimited tiers.** Start on Free at ₹0, move to Starter at ₹249/month, or unlock Unlimited at ₹449/month — flat for your workspace, not per user.
2. **Real individual accounts for everyone.** Assign proper permissions, round-robin rules and personal dashboards to each team member.
3. **Predictable unit economics.** Scale lead capture and team size without worrying about seat math every month.
