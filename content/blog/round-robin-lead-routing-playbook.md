# Round-Robin Lead Routing: Stop Leads From Waiting Unassigned

Most response-time delays do not happen because reps are slow to talk — they happen because nobody knows who owns the new lead. When enquiries sit in a shared spreadsheet or group chat waiting to be claimed, minutes turn into hours.

## Why manual lead claiming breaks under volume

- **Cherry-picking slows everyone down.** Reps scan for familiar neighborhoods or larger budgets while other enquiries sit cold.
- **Shift gaps create blind spots.** Leads arriving during meetings or after hours stay unassigned until someone checks the sheet.
- **No clear accountability.** If a lead belongs to everyone, nobody feels responsible for the first callback.

## Choosing the right routing rule

1. **Round-robin distribution.** Rotate incoming leads evenly across active reps so workload stays fair and response SLA stays predictable.
2. **Team and capacity routing.** Direct leads from specific campaigns to dedicated teams while capping open assignments when a rep is at capacity.
3. **Instant push alerts on assignment.** Pair assignment with a mobile push notification, email or WhatsApp alert so the owner sees the lead within seconds.

## Keep the loop measurable

Track first-response time and pending follow-ups per rep on a shared dashboard. Once ownership is automatic, coaching shifts from chasing updates to improving conversation quality.
