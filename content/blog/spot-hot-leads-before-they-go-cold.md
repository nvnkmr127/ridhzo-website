# Hot Leads & Going Cold: How to Rescue Stalled Pipeline Deals

Winning teams spend just as much energy protecting existing pipeline as they do answering fresh enquiries. Without clear visibility into deal momentum, promising prospects fade out between calls and site visits.

## Why deals go cold quietly

- **Missing next actions.** A call ends with "let's talk next week," but no follow-up reminder gets saved on the lead record.
- **Stage clutter.** Too many leads pile up in mid-pipeline columns with no signal showing which ones engaged recently.
- **Late manager reviews.** Owners only spot stalled opportunities at the end of the month when targets look tight.

## Two lists that surface what matters today

1. **Hot Leads.** Keep high-intent buyers front and center so reps prioritize callbacks, quotes and booking confirmations before opening older tabs.
2. **Going Cold.** Automatically flag any active lead that has gone 14 days without contact so reps can send a quick WhatsApp check-in or close the loop cleanly.

## Turn pipeline visibility into a daily habit

Review overdue follow-ups and Going Cold counts every morning in your Today panel. Five minutes of pipeline hygiene each morning recovers deals you already paid to acquire.
