# When-If-Then Automations: Zero-Admin Lead Assignment & Alerts

Every repetitive admin step between a form submission and a real sales conversation costs your team momentum. Simple event-based rules remove manual sorting without needing custom engineering.

## What belongs in your first automation rule

- **When a lead arrives:** Trigger instantly from Meta Lead Ads, Google Lead Form Ads, web forms or webhooks.
- **If conditions match:** Route by source, campaign, city or custom field so enquiries reach the right rep or team immediately.
- **Then execute actions:** Assign an owner, send a welcome WhatsApp message, schedule the initial follow-up and enroll the lead in a sequence.

## Keep human judgment where it counts

Automations work best when they clear away data entry so reps start every call with complete context. Let rules handle routing, status updates and reminders while your team focuses on closing.

## Review automation outcomes weekly

Use your executive dashboard and audit log to confirm every rule fires reliably and leads never stall between stages.
