# Ridhzo for Clinics, Dental & Aesthetic Centres

## The reality
Patients enquire from Instagram, Google and your website — often late at night — and call the reception when it's busy. Missed calls and slow replies send them to the next clinic.

## How clinics use Ridhzo

### Let patients book themselves
Put your **booking page** in your Instagram bio and Google Business profile. Patients pick a consultation slot within your clinic hours, and the appointment appears with a confirmation sent automatically.

### Never lose a missed call
Connect your phone provider (with the WhatsApp Business API): when a call to reception is missed, Ridhzo sends the caller a WhatsApp right away ("Sorry we missed your call — we'll call you back shortly") and logs it on their record.

### Fewer no-shows
Appointments get a confirmation with the clinic's map link and automatic reminders **24 hours and 1 hour before**.

### Follow up after the consultation
A **sequence** sends aftercare information, treatment options and a review appointment reminder.

### Suggested stages
New → Appointment Booked → Visited → Treatment Started / Not Interested.

### Useful custom fields
Treatment of interest, Preferred doctor, Preferred time, First visit (checkbox).
