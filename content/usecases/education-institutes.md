# Ridhzo for Education & Coaching Institutes

## The reality
Admission season brings a flood of enquiries from Google, Instagram, education fairs and walk-ins. Counsellors can't call everyone quickly, parents compare several institutes, and leads get lost in Excel sheets.

## How institutes use Ridhzo

### Every enquiry in one place
Google Search lead forms, Instagram ads, your website form, and CSV files from education fairs — all in one inbox, with duplicates merged.

### The right counsellor, instantly
- **Team-based round-robin** — e.g. Hindi, Telugu and English-speaking counsellor teams, or by course.
- Counsellors see only their own leads and get a push alert for each new one.
- The app itself works in **English, Hindi and Telugu**.

### Campus visits and counselling sessions
Share your **booking page** so parents book a campus visit or counselling slot within your working hours. Confirmations and reminders go out automatically.

### Follow-up through the admission cycle
A **sequence**: course details → scholarship information → fee deadline reminder → counsellor call invitation. It stops when the student replies or enrols.

### Useful custom fields
Course interested, Qualification, City, Intake (month/year), Parent's phone, Scholarship required.

### Suggested stages
Enquiry → Counselling Done → Application Submitted → Fee Paid / Dropped.

## What management tracks
- Which campaign brings **enrolments**, not just enquiries (Source ROI)
- Counsellor leaderboard and response times
- Enquiries going cold before the deadline
