# Ridhzo for Insurance, Loan & Financial Advisors

## The reality
People comparing policies or loans online are wary of spam calls. A polite, personal WhatsApp builds trust faster than a cold call from an unknown number. And the real money is in renewals and follow-ups that are easy to forget.

## How advisors use Ridhzo

### A professional first message in seconds
Tap WhatsApp on the lead and send a ready template:
> Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for checking term insurance options with us. To share an accurate quote, could you confirm your age and the cover amount you're looking for?

Or tap **Draft with AI** for a reply in Hindi, Hinglish or English based on what the lead asked.

### Renewals and reviews on time
- Add custom fields like **Policy type**, **Sum assured**, **Renewal date** and **Documents received**.
- Set follow-ups for renewal dates, tax-saving season and document collection — your phone reminds you on time.
- Filter "Renewal date in the next 30 days" and save it as a view.

### Documents in one place
Keep KYC documents, quotes and policy PDFs as **attachments** on the lead.

### A complete history
Every note, call, message and status change is on the lead's timeline, and important changes are recorded in the audit log.

## Great for solo advisors
Start on the **Free** plan with your personal WhatsApp — no Business API needed. Import your existing contacts from Excel in minutes. Move to Starter when you add a teammate or need more than 300 leads.
