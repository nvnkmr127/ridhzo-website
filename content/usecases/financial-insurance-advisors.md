# Ridhzo for Financial & Insurance Advisors

## The Industry Reality
Financial planning, term life insurance, and loan approvals require high trust. Prospects comparing quotes online are anxious about spam calls. A professional, personalized WhatsApp touchpoint establishes credibility much faster than a cold phone call from an unknown number.

---

## How Financial Advisors Use Ridhzo

### 1. Professional First Touch
- Send customized policy comparison summaries directly on WhatsApp:
  > *"Hi Amit, thanks for checking term life coverage options on our website. To give you the most accurate premium quote, could you confirm your age and preferred coverage amount?"*
- Eliminates the friction of prospect call-screening.

### 2. Scheduled Renewal & Policy Review Cadences
- Set automated delayed reminders for policy anniversary dates, tax-saving season follow-ups, or document collection.
- BullMQ delayed jobs trigger alerts on the advisor's phone when client check-ins are due.

### 3. Chronological Audit Trail
- Every note, policy stage change, and interaction is timestamped in PostgreSQL for compliance and relationship history.
